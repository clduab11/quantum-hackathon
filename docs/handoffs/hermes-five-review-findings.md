## Confirmed corrections to the supplied analysis

1. A 10% cut to one task's cost multiplies composite score by `0.9**(-1/3) = 1.0357441686512863`, not `1.1**(1/3) = 1.0322801154563672`. The latter is a 10% improvement in that task's ratio. These are hypothetical mathematical effects, not measured candidate scores.
2. Geometric-mean algebra does not prove single-task improvement impossible. `docs/decisions/D-PRA313-01.md` explicitly limits its negative result to one coefficient layout/schedule and does not rule out other layouts, adders, synthesis or margins.
3. Finite qubit trades differ from derivatives: for hypothetical DQI `Q=889`, `T=440000`, saving 10 qubits allows 10068.324098773039 extra Toffolis at equal cost, versus the differential approximation 9898.762654668166. Hypothetical invsqrt `Q=63`, `C=1629.6`, saving 21 allows 814.8 additional C, not the differential 543.2.
4. Chemistry's 128-qubit cap is an individual ANGLE circuit-width cap, not a total-resource cap (`verifiers/dfthcsos/verify.py:132-134` in the competition package). The strict raw Lambda and gap thresholds do not establish a lambda_eff floor of 46.79.
